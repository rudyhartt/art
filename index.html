Behind the images✳

A world is made
one feeling at a time.

I make images that begin with characters and open into worlds.

My practice combines art direction, storytelling and AI-assisted digital image-making. I work across illustrated scenes, sculptural visions and experiments with material, using recurring characters to connect otherwise unfamiliar places.

LOVE is one thread through this work: a companion, a protagonist and an evolving presence. Alongside an expanding cast of original characters, she gives abstract ideas an expression, a body and a point of view.

I am interested in the space between the playful and the unsettling, the artificial and the intimate. These works are part of an ongoing effort to give that space a visual language.
