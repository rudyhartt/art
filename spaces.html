Spaces

Spaces explores how places can hold stories. These concepts for interiors, gathering places and creative environments bring architecture, light, materials and visual identity together to shape the way people meet, work and feel.

Project 01 · Concept study

RUDY’S™

RUDY’S™ imagines a creative flagship where culture is made and shared under one roof. A daylit clubhouse connects listening, broadcasting, live performance and the Vision Store, while the identity extends into objects, clothing and character sculptures. Magenta glass, dark textured surfaces, warm metal and sculptural lighting carry the same visual language from the street frontage to the interior. The renders explore a place that shifts from quiet work and conversation to music and collective energy.

Project 02 · Concept study

THE MAISONETTE

THE MAISONETTE reimagines a split-level home as a setting for art, music and everyday rituals. Tall windows draw light through a piano salon and an upper living gallery, linked by a sculptural staircase and translucent balustrades. Pale stone, tactile plaster and warm walnut meet amber glass, muted blue and small notes of coral. New abstract paintings give each room its own rhythm, while open sightlines connect spaces for gathering, reading, working and retreat. The concept balances the intimacy of a home with the openness of a gallery.
